In this post, I summarize insights from the Thailand Domestic Tourism EDA project.

## Revenue Trends

The dataset revealed seasonal peaks in tourism revenue, with major holidays driving spikes in accommodation expenses.

## Tourist Demographics

Analysis of visitor origins showed strong contributions from regional markets.

## Accommodation Expenses

Hotel and lodging expenses correlated strongly with tourist arrivals, confirming tourism's direct economic impact.
